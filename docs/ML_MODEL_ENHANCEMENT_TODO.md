## 📊 **DATASET SPECIFICATIONS**

### **Road Issue Categories**
- **Potholes**: Deep holes in road surface
- **Cracks**: Linear fractures in pavement
- **Debris**: Foreign objects on road
- **Signage**: Damaged or missing signs
- **Drainage**: Blocked or damaged drains
- **Lighting**: Non-functional street lights
- **Sidewalk**: Damaged pedestrian paths
- **Traffic**: Traffic signal issues
- **Construction**: Unfinished road work
- **Other**: Miscellaneous issues

### **Data Requirements**
- **Minimum Dataset Size**: 10,000 images per category
- **Image Resolution**: 224x224 minimum, 512x512 preferred
- **Image Formats**: JPEG, PNG, WebP
- **Annotation Format**: COCO, YOLO, Pascal VOC
- **Data Split**: 70% train, 15% validation, 15% test
